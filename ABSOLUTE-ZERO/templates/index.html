Governing Thermodynamic Equations

1. Dynamic Thermal Heat Balance (Lumped Parameter ODE):
C_thermal · (dT_in / dt) = Σ (A_i / R_i)·(T_amb(t) - T_in(t)) + Q_solar_in(t) - Q_ventilation(t)
2. Solar Irradiance & Energy Yield (W/m²):
I_sol(t) = I_peak · [sin(π·(t - 6)/12)]^1.2 · (1 + 0.04·(Alt/1000)) Q_absorbed = I_sol(t) · (1 - ρ_reflectivity) · F_shape_exposure

Q_cond = UA · (T_amb - T_in) | Q_conv = h_c · A · (T_surf - T_amb) | Q_rad = ε·σ·A·(T_sky⁴ - T_surf⁴)
